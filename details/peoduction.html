Description.

This module is a dedicated web application for managing production processes, offering real-time monitoring and detailed tracking of all stages from planning to final output.

Production Planning

Allows managers to organize and schedule production according to current orders.

Raw Material Consumption

Tracks the materials consumed during each production run and updates stock levels automatically.

Workshop & Employee Monitoring

Records production data by workshop, machine, and employee for detailed performance analysis.

Finished Products Recording

Enables the registration of completed products and their integration into the finished goods inventory.

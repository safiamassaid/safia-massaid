About

I carry a product from brief to release — not just the part that fits my job title.

I'm a computer-systems engineer with a Master's degree and three years building software end to end: backend architecture and database design, the interfaces on top of them, and the test campaigns and documentation that prove the result works.

Most of my projects have been for small teams where nobody hands you a finished specification. So I write the specification, plan the sprints, build the thing, test it, and document it. That range is the reason clients bring me in — one person accountable from the first meeting to the release note.
